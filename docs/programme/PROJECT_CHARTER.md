# Project Charter

**Status:** candidate  
**Project:** Constellation Writer

## Mission

Build a local-first professional writing workbench that writers choose for daily, sustained work because it combines a calm and serious editor with reliable manuscript assembly, brutal recall, deterministic compilation, recoverable files, and governed transformation.

## Primary users

- Longform authors and essayists.
- Researchers and research-led writers.
- Screenwriters and script developers.
- Content strategists and professional editors.
- Transmedia and franchise creators who need continuity without a graph-first workspace.

## Core outcomes

1. A writing surface worthy of long daily sessions.
2. A file-sovereign manuscript substrate that survives app failure and migration.
3. Deterministic assembly, compilation, and recovery.
4. Governed AI and automation that remain inspectable, attributable, and reversible.

## Product wedge

Scrivener-grade control without cognitive drag; Ulysses-grade calm without a low ceiling; plain-file durability without graph drift; action routing without capture-first product distortion.

## Initial boundary

The first product is the writer chair, manuscript machine, literal and structural recall, and trust substrate. Compendium, semantic retrieval, graph projection, collaboration, and transmedia operations are later layers.

## Decision principle

Every feature must pay rent in writer time saved, anxiety removed, control gained, or output reliability improved.
