# Vision and North Star

## Vision

Constellation Writer becomes the professional manuscript workbench writers trust for the full life of serious work: drafting, returning, restructuring, revising, compiling, recovering, and eventually governing continuity across related outputs.

The product should feel quiet at the writing surface and exceptionally disciplined underneath it. Its advanced systems remain summonable. They do not occupy the chair.

## North Star

> A writer can open any long-lived project, understand where the work is, resume the exact thread, change it safely, assemble it deterministically, and recover from failure without surrendering ownership to an account, cloud service, opaque database, or autonomous model.

## Sequenced horizon

### Horizon 1: Writer Chair

- Editor quality under long sessions.
- Instant return to recent context.
- Plain-text Sheets and local projects.
- Keyboard-first navigation.
- Reliable autosave and recovery.

### Horizon 2: Manuscript Machine

- Directed manuscript assembly.
- Alternate arrangements.
- Deterministic compile and QA.
- Professional handoff packages.
- Output source maps.

### Horizon 3: Recall Engine

- Literal, structural, temporal, and operational recall.
- Half-remembered passage recovery.
- Source and annotation recall.
- Derived semantic assistance with visible uncertainty.

### Horizon 4: Continuity Sidecar

- Entities, claims, evidence, scope, canon state, and conflicts.
- Manuscript-first continuity inspection.
- Publication-state comparison.

### Horizon 5: Governed Transformation

- AI, scripts, imports, and batch operations through reviewable Mutation Envelopes and PatchSessions.
- Reversible automation without silent authorship loss.

### Horizon 6: Constellation Workbench

- Multiple works, adaptations, editions, and transmedia outputs sharing governed identity, provenance, and publication state.

## North-star measures

- Writers voluntarily return for real daily work.
- Zero silent data-loss events in controlled failure tests.
- Core drafting remains available without network or account.
- Every canonical field has one authoritative owner.
- Every non-human canonical mutation is inspectable and reversible.
- A project restores from an archive without its original cache.
- Identical canonical inputs produce equivalent compile semantics.

## Boundary

The horizon is not the backlog. Later capabilities earn entry only after the earlier substrate and writing experience meet their gates.
