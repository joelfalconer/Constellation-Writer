# Primary User Profiles

These are workflow profiles, not decorative personas.

## The Longform Author

Works across 50,000 to 200,000 words, drafts in scenes or sections, rearranges structure frequently, needs reliable whole-manuscript assembly, and fears export surprises or lost work.

Critical jobs: resume quickly, draft calmly, reorder safely, compare revisions, compile cleanly, recover confidently.

## The Research-Led Writer

Maintains source material beside prose, needs citations and traceability, recalls half-remembered passages, and distinguishes evidence from interpretation.

Critical jobs: search literally and structurally, connect sources without turning prose into PKM maintenance, inspect provenance, produce reviewable outputs.

## The Professional Editor and Strategist

Works across articles, reports, books, and client deliverables, often under deadline and across alternate outputs.

Critical jobs: version safely, comment and revise, create alternate assemblies, export to external collaborators, preserve clean handoff packages.

## The Screenwriter or Script Developer

Needs scene-level structure, rapid navigation, script-aware text behavior, and exports compatible with specialist workflows.

Critical jobs: scene assembly, Fountain-compatible text, revision tracking, deterministic script export.

## The Transmedia Creator

Writes primary narrative work while maintaining characters, places, rules, timelines, and publication continuity across multiple outputs.

Critical jobs: keep the manuscript primary, inspect claims and conflicts, preserve canon scope, avoid wiki gardening.

## Shared professional conditions

- Long sessions and fatigue.
- Interruption and context reload.
- High cost of data loss.
- Need for keyboard fluency.
- Projects outliving particular applications.
- Mixed use of external editors, file sync, and publishing tools.
