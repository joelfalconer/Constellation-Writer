# Product Constitution

**Status:** candidate  
**Authority:** highest-order product contract after acceptance

## Article I: The editor is sovereign

The editor is the primary authorship surface. Navigation, metadata, recall, AI, continuity, compile, and analysis approach it as summonable instruments. They do not permanently occupy or visually demote the prose field.

## Article II: Durable truth is writer-owned

Manuscript prose, project structure, compile intent, Compendium records, and governed transformation history live in inspectable project files. The product must survive deletion of all derived indexes.

## Article III: Identity is independent of presentation

Object identity does not depend on filename, title, path, heading, or manuscript placement. Placement and revision identities remain distinct from object identity.

## Article IV: Manuscripts are directed assemblies

Sheets carry prose. Manuscript manifests carry order, nesting, inclusion, and structural role. Filesystem order is never manuscript order.

## Article V: Transformation is governed

AI, scripts, imports, migrations, batch operations, and recovery actions use a shared mutation contract. Non-human changes do not enter canonical state without the review required by their consequence.

## Article VI: Output is deterministic and explainable

Compile is a build pipeline. Identical canonical inputs and profile semantics produce equivalent output. Inclusion, transformation, warnings, and source mapping are inspectable.

## Article VII: Recovery is product behavior

Canonical writes are atomic. Conflicts preserve both versions. Destructive operations and migrations are guarded by recovery artifacts. Restore explains its consequences before applying them.

## Article VIII: Intelligence is derived

Search indexes, graph projections, embeddings, previews, statistics, and AI reports are derived or logged layers. They may enrich the workbench but may not become prerequisites for basic authorship or recovery.

## Article IX: Complexity must earn its place

Every feature must save writer time, reduce anxiety, increase control, improve recall, or increase output reliability. Complexity that cannot demonstrate one of these outcomes is deferred or rejected.

## Article X: The system remains honest

Candidate designs, inferred claims, AI-generated records, and untested architecture remain visibly provisional. Promotion requires provenance, validation, authority, and review.
