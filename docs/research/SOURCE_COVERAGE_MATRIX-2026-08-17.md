## Primary-source coverage rule

A current product claim is considered covered in this matrix only when:

1. the source is vendor-owned official documentation, help, knowledge-base, or product material;
2. the source is registered in `SOURCE_REGISTRY.yaml` with an access date;
3. the claim is represented by a current evidence unit or a dossier claim that points to one;
4. the wording does not upgrade documented capability into user sentiment, comparative superiority, or Constellation architecture truth.
