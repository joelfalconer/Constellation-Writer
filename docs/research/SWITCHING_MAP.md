Vendor documentation **cannot establish why users switch**, what they regret, or which friction becomes intolerable. ## What current evidence actually supports

- **Ulysses ↔ Scrivener is a meaningful comparison** because current docs show a Sheet/Group/library/export model on one side and a Binder/discrete-chunk/Compile model on the other. They do not show actual migration motives.
- **Drafts → manuscript tool is a meaningful workflow boundary** because Drafts documents capture/list/action/workspace mechanics rather than a Binder-style manuscript compile model. The direction of real user migration remains unmeasured.
- **Scrivener → Vellum/Atticus is a meaningful production boundary** because current docs show manuscript compilation on the Scrivener side and publication-oriented formatting/output on the Vellum/Atticus side. No claim is made about frequency or satisfaction.
- **Obsidian → manuscript tool is a meaningful architecture boundary** because Obsidian documents local Markdown, links, graph, properties, and rebuildable metadata, but this does not establish that its users experience manuscript friction.

## Switching risks for Constellation Writer

These remain **design risks**, not market findings:

- Too much schema before the first useful sentence.
- File sovereignty exposed as folder administration.
- Compile power arriving with excessive cognitive weight.
- AI governance becoming bureaucratic instead of reassuring.
- Compendium scope turning the product into a second app.
- Cross-platform editor/shell differences undermining trust.

## Required user-evidence pass

Before any switching claim becomes `source_assertion`, gather switcher evidence with exact locators and record:

1. starting tool/version and professional workflow;
2. precipitating incident rather than generic preference;
3. chronic irritation versus critical failure;
4. replacement quality sought;
5. tradeoff consciously accepted;
6. paired-tool workflow retained after switching;
7. whether the switch persisted after 30+ days.

Until then, switching remains a map of **questions worth asking**, not a map of observed migration.
