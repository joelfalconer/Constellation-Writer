## Product thesis

The current corpus characterizes Scrivener as the strongest benchmark for project containment, binder-scale structure, research adjacency, and compile control.

## Core professional workflows

- Break a long work into scenes, chapters, notes, and research.
- Manipulate structure independently of prose.
- Keep supporting materials adjacent to the manuscript.
- Compile a whole work from many parts.

## Atomic affordances to benchmark

- binder reorder, indent, outdent, include/exclude;
- manuscript/research boundary;
- split and merge operations;
- compile-role assignment;
- navigation from structural tree back to writing position.

## Borrow / reject / test

**Borrow:** binder control, chunked authoring, research adjacency, compile as a first-class activity.

**Reject:** architecture or UI complexity that makes the binder compete with the editor.

**Test:** whether a manifest-first file model can preserve Scrivener-class structural control with lower cognitive and recovery cost.

## Unknowns

Exact current-version behavior, long-session ergonomics, import/export loss cases, and accessibility observations require a dedicated product study.
