## Product thesis

The current corpus treats iA Writer as the strongest editor-as-instrument benchmark, especially for syntax/style attention and provenance-oriented writing behavior.

## Atomic affordances to benchmark

- typography and measure;
- focus/syntax visibility;
- selection and caret fidelity;
- style feedback that does not become dashboard residue;
- clean source presentation before and after analysis overlays.

## Borrow / reject / test

**Borrow:** editorial restraint, writing-surface seriousness, summonable analysis.

**Reject:** any analysis treatment that distracts from sentence-level authorship.

**Test:** Draft and Revise modes against fatigue, selection behavior, and overlay removal.

## Unknowns

Current corpus does not yet contain exact locators or measured current-version accessibility and latency behavior.
