## Role in the benchmark set

Obsidian is primarily useful here as a boundary case for file ownership, linking, plugins, and graph-oriented knowledge work. The current research conclusion is that graph functionality is useful as a projection but should not become the manuscript store or default writing surface.

## Borrow / reject / test

**Borrow:** inspectable files, durable internal links, extensible mental model where extension does not own core writing.

**Reject:** graph-first navigation, plugin dependency for foundational writing, vault gardening as a prerequisite for manuscript work.

**Test:** derived backlinks and graph projections only after literal/structural recall and manuscript assembly are excellent.

## Unknowns

The present evidence unit is architectural rather than a full product dossier. Current Obsidian behavior, plugin ecology, sync, accessibility, and professional-writing complaints require dedicated evidence collection.
