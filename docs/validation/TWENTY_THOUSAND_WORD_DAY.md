# 20,000-Word Day Protocol

## Objective

Stress the writing surface and substrate under an extreme but plausible high-volume production day.

## Method

Use either a consenting professional writer performing real work or a controlled mixed protocol combining authentic drafting, dictated insertion, paste/import, revision, and structural operations. Synthetic typing alone is insufficient for cognitive findings.

## Work blocks

- 8 × 75-minute writing blocks.
- 15-minute interruption between blocks.
- Structural reorganization after blocks 3 and 6.
- Project search after every block.
- Draft compile after blocks 2, 4, 6, and 8.
- Forced app restart after block 5.
- Derived-index rebuild during block 7.

## Record

- words added and removed;
- save and snapshot events;
- p95 keystroke latency;
- memory and CPU trend;
- Sheet switch latency;
- errors, scroll jumps, and lost selections;
- time to resume after interruption;
- reported fatigue and trust;
- final archive and restore validation.

## Pass criteria

- no acknowledged text lost;
- no sustained keystroke latency above 32 ms;
- no core operation blocked by a background index;
- every compile explains warnings and inclusion;
- post-restart recovery restores the intended context;
- project archive restores and recompiles.
