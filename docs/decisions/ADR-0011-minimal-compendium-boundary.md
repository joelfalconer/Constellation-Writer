# ADR-0011: Keep Compendium minimal until the writing substrate passes

- Status: proposed
- Decision class: product-sequencing, high scope impact
- Review gate: F0/F1

## Decision

Design stable Entity, Claim, Evidence, and Conflict contracts now, but limit early product implementation to:

- manual entity stubs;
- manual or observed claims;
- evidence anchors;
- current-Sheet Inspector lookup;
- basic conflict records.

Defer graph exploration, broad automated extraction, motif maps, franchise dashboards, and autonomous continuity repair.

## Rationale

The Compendium earns credibility only after the editor, vault, manuscript, compile, and recovery loop is excellent. Early broad implementation risks transforming the product into a second-brain or worldbuilding application.

## Revisit trigger

The Writer Chair and Manuscript Machine pass their gates, and user research demonstrates a continuity problem that the minimal boundary cannot solve.
